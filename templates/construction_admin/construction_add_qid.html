Add Staff QID

Add and manage Qatar ID information for a construction staff member.

Qatar ID Information

Enter the staff member's Qatar ID information accurately. The expiry date will be used by the system to determine when reminder notifications should be sent.

Staff Information
Enter the staff member's 11-digit Qatar ID number.
QID Validity
The date on which the Qatar ID was issued.
This date is required because it controls the reminder system.
QID Document

The document will be securely uploaded to Cloudinary and the Cloudinary URL will be stored in the database. Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG.

Staff Contact
QID expiry reminders will be sent to this email address.
The manager will also receive QID expiry reminder notifications.
Additional Information
Automatic Reminder Notifications

Once the QID record is saved, the system will use the expiry date to determine when reminder emails should be sent to the staff member, manager and designated administrator.
